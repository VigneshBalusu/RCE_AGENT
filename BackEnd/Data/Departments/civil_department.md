# Department of Civil Engineering (CE) - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of Civil Engineering Department
* **Keywords:** Civil Engineering, Civil Profile, Civil Establishment, Civil Seats, Civil Intake, CE Dept, Infrastructure Development, Structural Design

## 1. Department Profile & Stats
* **Keywords:** Civil Engineering Department, Established 2008, BTech Civil Seats, Intake Capacity, Green Building Technologies
* **Overview:** Established in 2008, the Civil Engineering (CE) department has an intake capacity of 60 seats for the B.Tech (UG) program, focusing on Infrastructure Development, Structural Design, Surveying, and Green Building Technologies.

## 2. Vision & Mission
* **Keywords:** Civil Vision, Civil Mission, Civil Goals, Innovative Solutions, Global Competence, Skill Development
* **Vision:** To provide quality education and produce skilled manpower for innovative solutions in Civil Engineering to handle societal challenges.
* **Mission:** To impart globally competent quality education, provide skill and application-based training for employment and higher education, and encourage students to develop innovative solutions for societal Civil Engineering problems.

## 4. Academic Programs Offered
* **Keywords:** Civil Courses, Civil Degree, BTech Civil, Structural Engineering, Geotechnical Engineering
* **B.Tech Program:** Offers a 4-year B.Tech in Civil Engineering with a comprehensive focus on Structural Engineering, Geotechnical Engineering, Transportation, Water Resources, and Environmental Science.

## 5. Laboratories & Infrastructure
* **Keywords:** Civil Labs, Survey Lab, Concrete Lab, Civil Infrastructure, Hydraulics Lab, Geotechnical Lab, CAD GIS Lab
* **Key Laboratories:** State-of-the-art facilities include the Surveying Lab (Total Stations, Theodolites, Dumpy levels), Concrete Technology Lab (Compression Testing Machine), Geotechnical/Soil Mechanics Lab (Direct shear test, Sieve shakers), Transportation Engineering Lab (Bitumen/Aggregate testing), Environmental Engineering Lab (Water quality analysis), CAD/GIS Lab (AutoCAD, ArcGIS), Strength of Materials Lab (UTM), and Engineering Geology Lab (mineral/rock identification).

## 6. Student Clubs & Activities
* **Keywords:** Civil Clubs, RACE Association, Abhigyan Newsletter, Civil Events, Model Expo, Engineers Day
* **Associations & Events:** The Royal Association of Civil Engineering (RACE) serves as the official student body organizing technical fests, Model Expos displaying advanced structures, and Engineer’s Day celebrations with technical quizzes. The department also publishes the "ABHIGYAN" newsletter showcasing student research and conducts hands-on workshops on Total Station and Revit Architecture.

## 7. Placements (Civil Specific)
* **Keywords:** Civil Placements, Civil Jobs, Core Companies, Civil Salary, Placement Stats Civil, Civil Recruiters
* **Placement Statistics:** The department places 30 to 45 students annually in both core construction and IT firms. The average package ranges from 2.0 LPA to 3.0 LPA, with highest packages reaching 4.5 LPA in the IT sector and 2.4 LPA to 3.0 LPA in the Core sector.
* **Top Recruiters:** Core construction recruiters include GC Infra Pvt Ltd, Jawani Infra Developers, Trends Infratech, and Stails Design. IT and software recruiters hiring civil students with coding skills include Pie-Infocomm, DXC Technology, Wipro, and TCS.
