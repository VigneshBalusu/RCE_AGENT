# Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of ECE Department
* **Keywords:** Electronics and Communication Engineering, ECE Profile, ECE Establishment, ECE Seats, ECE Intake, VLSI Design, NBA Accredited

## 1. Department Profile & Stats
* **Keywords:** ECE Department, Established 2008, BTech ECE Seats, MTech VLSI Seats, Embedded Systems, Signal Processing
* **Overview:** Established in 2008 and NBA Accredited, the ECE department has an intake capacity of 180 seats for B.Tech (UG) and 6 seats for M.Tech (PG) specializing in VLSI Design. Key strengths include a strong focus on VLSI, Embedded Systems, and Signal Processing supported by dedicated research facilities.

## 2. Vision & Mission
* **Keywords:** ECE Vision, ECE Mission, ECE Goals, Innovative Graduates, Technical Education, Cutting Edge Research, Holistic Growth
* **Vision:** To produce innovative, ethical, and socially responsible graduates in the field of Electronics and Communication Engineering who can address global and environmental challenges through excellence in technical education and research.
* **Mission:** To provide a creative learning environment with state-of-the-art infrastructure, engage in cutting-edge VLSI and Embedded research through industry collaborations, and imbibe a self-learning attitude, entrepreneurial skills, and professional ethics to make graduates globally competitive.

## 3. Leadership & Faculty
* **Keywords:** ECE HOD, Head of ECE, Dr Bonam Raghavaiah, Contact ECE HOD, VLSI Architectures, Hybrid Parallel Prefix Adders, ECE Board of Studies
* **Details:** The department is headed by Dr. Bonam Raghavaiah (Contact: hod-ece@rcee.ac.in). An expert in Hybrid Parallel Prefix Adders and VLSI Architectures, he has authored 30+ research papers and 2 textbooks on Electronic Devices & Circuits, holds 5 patents, and serves as the Chairman of the ECE Board of Studies.

## 4. Academic Programs Offered
* **Keywords:** ECE Courses, ECE Degree, BTech ECE, MTech VLSI, Analog Electronics, Digital Communications, Chip Design, FPGA Implementation
* **Programs Offered:** The department offers a 4-year B.Tech in ECE focusing on Analog Electronics, Digital Communications, Antenna Theory, and IoT, alongside a 2-year M.Tech in VLSI Design focusing on Chip Design, FPGA Implementation, and Low Power VLSI.

## 5. Laboratories & Infrastructure
* **Keywords:** ECE Labs, VLSI Lab, DSP Lab, Embedded Lab, ECE Infrastructure, Xilinx, Mentor Graphics, MATLAB, Microprocessors Lab
* **Facilities:** The department features 8+ specialized laboratories equipped with licensed software like Xilinx, Mentor Graphics, and MATLAB. Key facilities include the VLSI Design Lab (Spartan-3 FPGA Kits), Microprocessors & Microcontrollers Lab (8086 & 8051 kits, Keil µVision, ARM processors), Digital Signal Processing (DSP) Lab (TMS320C6713 kits), Electronic Devices & Circuits (EDC) Lab, AC & DC Communications Lab (Spectrum Analyzers, MULTISIM), STLD / PLC Lab for automation, and a dedicated R&D Lab for innovation projects.

## 6. Student Clubs & Professional Chapters
* **Keywords:** ECE Clubs, HUNT Association, IETE Chapter, ISTE Chapter, ECE Events, Circuit Idea Expos
* **Associations & Events:** The primary student association is "HUNT" (Home of Unlimited Novel Technologies), established in 2010 to encourage student participation through technical quizzes, circuit idea expos, and poster presentations. The department also hosts an active IETE Student Chapter for emerging electronics trends and an ISTE Student Chapter focusing on leadership and career readiness.
