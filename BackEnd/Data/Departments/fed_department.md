# Department of Freshman Engineering (FED) - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of FED Department
* **Keywords:** Freshman Engineering, FED Profile, Science and Humanities, First Year, S&H Dept, Induction Program

## 1. Department Profile & Stats
* **Keywords:** FED Department, Science & Humanities, I-Year Curriculum, First Year Engineering, Basic Sciences
* **Overview:** The Freshman Engineering Department (FED), also known as Science & Humanities (S&H), handles the First Year (I-Year) curriculum for all B.Tech branches. It acts as a crucial bridge between Intermediate/10+2 education and Core Engineering, focusing on fundamental sciences and discipline.

## 2. Vision & Mission
* **Keywords:** FED Vision, FED Mission, S&H Goals, Technical Education, Holistic Development, Bridge Courses, Induction Programs
* **Vision:** To emerge as a Centre of Excellence offering high-quality Technical Education and Research Opportunities to learners and also develop the complete personality of graduates with good communication, discipline, and ethics.
* **Mission:** To mould students through methodological teaching for core engineering subjects, bring intellectual thinking and individuality into their minds, and facilitate a smooth transition from school-level learning to professional engineering education through Bridge Courses and Induction Programs.

## 4. Academic Focus & Curriculum (I-Year)
* **Keywords:** First Year Syllabus, Engineering Physics, Engineering Chemistry, M1, M2, CPDS, Engineering Graphics, Communicative English
* **Core Curriculum:** The first-year syllabus covers Mathematics (Linear Algebra/M1, Calculus/M2, Probability & Statistics), Physics (Applied Physics, branch-specific Semiconductor Physics), Engineering Chemistry, Communicative English & Soft Skills, C Programming & Data Structures (CPDS), and Engineering Graphics (Drawing and Visualization).

## 5. Laboratories & Infrastructure
* **Keywords:** FED Labs, English Lab, Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, Engineering Workshop, IT Workshop, K-Van Solutions, Globerena
* **Facilities:** The department maintains state-of-the-art basic science labs including the English Communication Skills (ELCS) Lab (licensed "K-Van Solutions" and "Globerena" software for listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills), Engineering Physics Lab (optics, lasers, semiconductors), Engineering Chemistry Lab (water analysis, titrations), Engineering Workshop (hands-on training in Carpentry, Fitting, House Wiring, Tin Smithy), and an IT Workshop (PC Hardware assembly, OS Installation, MS Office training).

## 6. Student Support & Counseling
* **Keywords:** FED Counseling, Mentoring System, Parent Communication, Discipline, Student Support
* **System & Discipline:** The department assigns a dedicated Faculty Mentor for every 20-30 students to monitor attendance, academic progress, and disciplinary issues. FED enforces strict discipline (Dress code, ID cards, Punctuality) and maintains regular communication with parents regarding attendance and exam marks via SMS and phone.

## 7. Key Events: "PARICHAY" (Induction Program)
* **Keywords:** Parichay, Orientation Day, Induction Program, Freshers Day, Zero Semester, Universal Human Values
* **Activities:** The annual orientation program, "PARICHAY", welcomes new students and parents to the college culture, autonomous rules, and placement achievements. It features a 2-3 week "Zero Semester" Induction Program covering Yoga, Creative Arts, Universal Human Values (UHV), literary activities, and motivational guest lectures by eminent personalities.
