# Department of CSE - Internet of Things (IoT) - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of CSE IoT Department
* **Keywords:** CSE IoT, Internet of Things Profile, IoT Seats, IoT Intake, Emerging Tech, IoT Specialization

## 1. Department Profile & Stats
* **Keywords:** CSE IoT Department, Established 2020, BTech IoT Seats, Emerging Technology Branch
* **Overview:** Introduced as an emerging technology branch in 2020, the CSE - Internet of Things (IoT) specialization functions under the Department of Computer Science & Engineering with an intake capacity of 60 seats for the B.Tech (UG) program.

## 2. Vision & Mission
* **Keywords:** IoT Vision, IoT Mission, IoT Goals, Hardware Software Integration, Sensor Technology, Smart Solutions, Industry Readiness
* **Vision:** To develop competent professionals in the field of Internet of Things (IoT) with a strong foundation in hardware-software integration, data analytics, and sensor technology to solve societal problems.
* **Mission:** To provide in-depth technical expertise in Embedded Systems, Sensors, and Cloud Computing, encourage innovative smart solutions for Smart Cities, Agriculture, and Healthcare, and train students in industry-standard tools like Raspberry Pi, Arduino, and AWS IoT.

## 3. Leadership & Contact
* **Keywords:** IoT HOD, IoT Coordinator, Contact IoT, Dr B Narendra, CSE Head
* **Details:** Functioning under the guidance of the CSE Head of Department, the IoT program is headed by Dr. B. Narendra (Contact: hod-cse@rcee.ac.in or specialized ID hod-iot@rcee.ac.in), with dedicated professors coordinating academic activities and labs.

## 4. Academic Focus & Curriculum
* **Keywords:** IoT Syllabus, IoT Subjects, IoT Curriculum, Embedded Systems, Sensor Technology, Communication Protocols, Cloud Data
* **Core Curriculum:** The specialized syllabus covers IoT Architecture (Perception, Network, Application layers), Embedded Systems (Microcontrollers like 8051 and ARM, RTOS), Sensor Technology (Temperature, Proximity, Gas, Motion sensors), Communication Protocols (MQTT, CoAP, Zigbee, Bluetooth BLE, LoRaWAN), and Cloud & Data (AWS IoT Core, Python for Data Science, Big Data Analytics).

## 5. Laboratories & Infrastructure
* **Keywords:** IoT Labs, Sensors Lab, Arduino Lab, Embedded IoT Lab, Raspberry Pi, AWS IoT, Smart Home Automation
* **Facilities:** Bridging hardware and software, the department features the IoT Sensors & Devices Lab (Arduino Uno, NodeMCU ESP8266, and Raspberry Pi 4 kits interfacing with DHT11, Ultrasonic, and IR sensors), Embedded Systems Lab (ARM Cortex Boards, Keil µVision IDE), Advanced Python & Data Science Lab (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib), Cloud Computing Lab (AWS/Azure remote monitoring), and an Innovation/Project Lab for building prototypes like Smart Home Automation and Smart Irrigation Systems.

## 6. Student Activities & Events
* **Keywords:** IoT Events, IoT Projects, Hackathons, Smart India Hackathon, Smart Agriculture, Industrial Automation
* **Activities:** Students engage in regular hands-on workshops on Building Smart Devices and Robotics Integration, actively participate in the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Hardware Edition, and showcase Smart Agriculture and Industrial Automation prototypes at annual Project Expos.

## 7. Placements & Career Scope
* **Keywords:** IoT Jobs, IoT Placements, IoT Salary, IoT Roles, IoT Developer, Embedded Systems Engineer, Cloud Solution Architect
* **Top Recruiters & Roles:** Graduates are hired for roles such as IoT Developer (Firmware/Software), Embedded Systems Engineer, Cloud Solution Architect, and Network Engineer. Top recruiters include core hardware companies (Efftronics, Medha Servo Drives, Smart SoC, MosChip), software MNCs (TCS IoT Wing, Wipro Industrial Automation, Cognizant, Accenture), and high-demand AgriTech and HealthTech startups.
