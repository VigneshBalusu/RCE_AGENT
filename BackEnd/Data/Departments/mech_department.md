# Department of Mechanical Engineering (ME) - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of Mechanical Engineering Department
* **Keywords:** Mechanical Engineering, ME Profile, Mechanical Establishment, Mechanical Seats, Mechanical Intake, Machine Design, Centre of Excellence

## 1. Department Profile & Stats
* **Keywords:** Mechanical Engineering Department, Established 2011, BTech Mechanical Seats, MTech Machine Design Seats, Manufacturing, Robotics, Hyundai Woosu Ties
* **Overview:** Established in 2011, the Mechanical Engineering (ME) department is recognized as a Centre of Excellence in Manufacturing and Robotics. It has an intake capacity of 60 seats for B.Tech (UG) and 6 seats for M.Tech (PG) specializing in Machine Design. The department maintains strong industry ties with Hyundai and Woosu Automotive.

## 2. Vision & Mission
* **Keywords:** Mechanical Vision, Mechanical Mission, Mechanical Goals, Technical Education, Innovative Skills, Life Long Learning, Leadership Skills
* **Vision:** To become a Centre of Excellence in Mechanical Engineering by providing quality technical education and research to learners and solving social and environmental problems by developing innovative and creative skills.
* **Mission:** To empower students with innovative research skills for the mechanical field, produce graduates who are solution providers with a lifelong learning attitude, and equip learners with ethical responsibilities and leadership skills.

## 4. Academic Programs Offered
* **Keywords:** Mechanical Courses, Mechanical Degree, BTech Mechanical, MTech Machine Design, Thermal Engineering, Auto CAD, Industrial Automation
* **Programs Offered:** The department offers a 4-year B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering focusing on Robotics, Thermal Engineering, Auto-CAD, and Industrial Automation, alongside a 2-year M.Tech in Machine Design focusing on Advanced CAD/CAM, Vibrations, and Material Science.

## 5. Laboratories & Infrastructure
* **Keywords:** Mechanical Labs, Thermal Lab, Machine Tools Lab, CAD CAM Lab, Heat Transfer Lab, Fluid Mechanics, Production Technology, SolidWorks, ANSYS, CATIA
* **Facilities:** The department features 10+ specialized labs, including the Thermal Engineering Lab (IC Engine testing), Machine Tools Lab (Lathe, Milling, Drilling), and a CAD/CAM Lab equipped with SolidWorks, ANSYS (16.0), CATIA (V5), and AutoCAD (2023). Other facilities include the Heat Transfer Lab, Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulic Machines, Production Technology Lab, and a dedicated Robotics Lab focused on Humanoid Robotics and Automation.

## 6. Student Clubs & Professional Chapters
* **Keywords:** Mechanical Clubs, ISTE Chapter, IAENG, SDIWC, Professional Societies, Project Expo, Design Thinking, Go Karting
* **Associations & Events:** Students participate in active chapters for ISTE, IAENG, and SDIWC. Key activities include "Project Expos" featuring student-built Go-Karts and robots, as well as specialized "Design Thinking" workshops.

* **Keywords:** Mechanical Placements, Core Mechanical Jobs, Mechanical Salary, Automotive Industry, Hyundai Jobs, Raam Group, Woosu Automotive, Lokesh Machine Tools, Kapston, Voltech, Accenture, TCS Ninja
