# Academic Regulations & Examination Rules - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of Academic Regulations
* **Keywords:** Academic Rules, Exam Rules, Regulations Overview, RCEE Guidelines, Pass Marks, Attendance Shortage, Credits, Backlogs

## 1. Applicable Regulations (Batch-Wise)
* **Keywords:** R23 Regulation, R20 Regulation, Batch Rules, Syllabus Norms
* **R23 Regulation:** Applicable for students admitted from 2023 onwards (Current 1st & 2nd Years).
* **R20 Regulation:** Applicable for senior batches (Current 3rd & 4th Years).

## 2. Attendance Requirements
* **Keywords:** Attendance, Attendance Shortage, Condonation, Detention, Medical Leave
* **Minimum Target:** 75% aggregate attendance is mandatory to appear for End Exams.
* **Condonation (Medical):** Shortage between 65% and 75% may be condoned with a Medical Certificate and condonation fee.
* **Detention:** Attendance below 65% generally leads to detention, requiring the student to repeat the semester.

## 3. Pass Criteria & Grading (10-Point Scale)
* **Keywords:** Pass Marks, Pass Criteria, Grading System, Internal Marks, External Marks
* **External Exam:** Minimum 35% marks required in the End-Semester exam.
* **Total Marks:** Minimum 40% combined aggregate (Internal + External) required to pass the subject.

## 4. Examination Pattern
* **Keywords:** Mid Exams, End Exams, Supplementary Exams, Supply Exams, Internal Assessment
* **Internals:** Conducted twice per semester, including both descriptive and objective tests.
* **End-Semester:** 3-hour duration exams conducted at the end of the term.
* **Supplementary:** Conducted alongside regular exams for students who need to clear failed subjects.

## 5. Credit Structure (B.Tech)
* **Keywords:** Credits, Total Credits, Degree Requirements, BTech Credits
* **Total Credits:** 160 Credits are required for the award of a B.Tech Degree.
* **Distribution:** Includes Basic Sciences, Professional Core, Open Electives, and Projects.

## 6. Promotion Rules (Credit Detention)
* **Keywords:** Promotion Rules, Credit Detention, Academic Promotion, Detention Rules
* **Year 1 to 2:** Promotion is based on attendance only.
* **Year 2 to 3:** Must secure a minimum of 50% of total credits up to 2nd Year 1st Sem.
* **Year 3 to 4:** Must secure a minimum of 50% of total credits up to 3rd Year 1st Sem.

## 7. Academic Calendar
* **Keywords:** Academic Calendar, Exam Dates, Mid Dates, Holidays, Summer Vacation
* **Status:** The academic calendar is dynamic and changes annually to outline specific exam dates, holidays, and vacations for the current academic year.
