# Admissions Process - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of RCEE Admissions
* **Keywords:** Admissions, Enrollment, Join RCEE, Admission Guide, Admission Process, RCEE Steps

## Admission Pathways & Quotas
* **Keywords:** Category A, Category B, Government Quota, Management Quota, Admission Quota
* **Quotas:** Admissions are divided into AP EAMCET (Category A – Government Quota) and Management Quota (Category B – Direct Admission).

## AP EAMCET Counseling Code
* **Keywords:** Counseling Code, RACE Code, EAMCET Code, EAPCET Code, College Code
* **Official Code:** The AP EAMCET counseling code for Ramachandra College of Engineering is RACE.

## Step 1: Admission Eligibility Criteria
* **Keywords:** Eligibility, Admission Requirements, BTech Eligibility, MPC Marks
* **Criteria:** Candidates must complete 10+2 with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry. AP EAMCET qualification is required for Category A, while Category B requires minimum academic eligibility.

## Step 2: Entrance Examination (AP EAMCET)
* **Keywords:** EAMCET Exam, APSCHE Portal, EAPCET Rank, Entrance Test
* **Process:** Register for AP EAMCET (EAPCET) through the official APSCHE portal, appear for the examination, and obtain a valid rank.

## Step 3: Counseling Registration Process
* **Keywords:** Counseling Registration, Certificate Verification, Registration Fee
* **Process:** Register on the AP EAMCET counseling portal, pay the counseling registration fee, and complete certificate verification.

## Step 4: Choice Filling (Web Options)
* **Keywords:** Web Options, Choice Filling, Lock Choices, Option Entry
* **Process:** Select Ramachandra College of Engineering using the code RACE and carefully lock choices before the official deadline.

## Step 5: Seat Allotment Results
* **Keywords:** Seat Allotment, Allotment Order, Allotment Results
* **Process:** Seat allotment results are published online by APSCHE. Students must download the allotment order immediately after confirmation.

## Step 6: Reporting to College
* **Keywords:** College Reporting, Admission Fees, Campus Admission, Document Verification
* **Process:** Report to the RCEE campus with the allotment order to complete document verification and pay tuition and admission fees.

## Documents Required for Counseling
* **Keywords:** Required Documents, Admission Checklist, Certificates, TC, Marks Memo
* **Required Files:** AP EAMCET Hall Ticket & Rank Card, 10th (SSC) & Intermediate (10+2) Marks Memos, Transfer Certificate (TC), Caste/Income Certificates (if applicable), Aadhaar Card, and Passport-size photographs.

## Admission Important Dates & Links
* **Keywords:** Admission Dates, Admission Links, RCEE Website, APSCHE Portal
* **Official Links:** RCEE Admissions Page (https://www.rcee.ac.in/admissions) and the APSCHE Counseling Authority.
