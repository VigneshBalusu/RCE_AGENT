# Admissions Process - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Complete Admission Process (Step-by-Step Guide)
* **Keywords:** Admissions, Enrollment, Join RCEE, Admission Guide, Admission Process, RCEE Steps, how to apply, how to join, admission procedure, apply for admission, Category A, Category B, Government Quota, Management Quota, Admission Quota, Counseling Code, RACE Code, EAMCET Code, EAPCET Code, College Code
* **Quotas:** Admissions are divided into AP EAMCET (Category A – Government Quota) and Management Quota (Category B – Direct Admission).
* **AP EAMCET Counseling Code:** The official code for Ramachandra College of Engineering is **RACE**.
* **Step 1 — Check Eligibility:** Candidates must complete 10+2 (Intermediate) with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC). AP EAMCET qualification is required for Category A (Convener Quota), while Category B (Management Quota) requires minimum academic eligibility.
* **Step 2 — Appear for Entrance Exam (AP EAMCET / EAPCET):** Register for AP EAMCET (EAPCET) through the official APSCHE portal, appear for the examination, and obtain a valid rank.
* **Step 3 — Counseling Registration:** Register on the AP EAMCET counseling portal, pay the counseling registration fee, and complete certificate verification.
* **Step 4 — Choice Filling (Web Options):** Select Ramachandra College of Engineering using the code **RACE** and carefully lock your choices before the official deadline.
* **Step 5 — Seat Allotment Results:** Seat allotment results are published online by APSCHE. Students must download the allotment order immediately after confirmation.
* **Step 6 — Report to College:** Report to the RCEE campus with the allotment order to complete document verification and pay tuition and admission fees.
