# Campus Infrastructure & Facilities - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of Campus Infrastructure
* **Keywords:** Infrastructure Overview, Campus Facilities, Amenities Guide, RCEE Resources, Campus Map Info, Academic Infrastructure, Recreational Facilities

## 1. Laboratories (Academic & Research)
* **Keywords:** Labs, Laboratories, Research Labs, Computer Labs, ECE Labs, CSE Labs, Practical Exposure
* **Facilities:** RCEE features 70+ state-of-the-art laboratories for coursework, mini-projects, and research. 
* **Specialized Labs:** Core departments have dedicated spaces, including ECE (VLSI), EEE (Power Systems), Mechanical (CNC/Workshop), and Civil (Surveying). 
* **Computer Labs:** High-configuration systems (i5/i7) support CSE, AI-DS, and Cyber Security programs.

## 2. Central Library (Knowledge Resource Center)
* **Keywords:** Central Library, Books, E-Journals, Digital Library, NPTEL, Reading Hall, DELNET, KNIMBUS
* **Overview:** The Central Library is fully automated with barcode and biometric systems.
* **Physical Resources:** The library houses 27,950+ volumes, 3,550+ unique titles, and 90+ National/International print journals.
* **Digital Resources:** Students have remote access via DELNET and KNIMBUS, with E-Journals from IEEE, Springer, and ASCE. 
* **Facilities:** Features a 300-seat Reading Hall, a Digital Library equipped with 20+ systems, and NPTEL Video Course access.

## 3. Innovation Ecosystem (R&D)
* **Keywords:** IDEA Lab, Startups, Innovation, Entrepreneurship, IEDC, Patents, Product Development
* **AICTE IDEA Lab:** A prestigious government-funded lab (₹1.1 Crore grant) open 24/7 for student innovators focusing on prototyping and product development.
* **IEDC:** The Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Cell supports student startups, funding, and patent filing assistance.

## 4. Sports & Fitness
* **Keywords:** Sports, Gym, Fitness, Games, Cricket, Volleyball, Basketball, Indoor Games
* **Outdoor Sports:** Sprawling cricket ground, volleyball courts, football field, and basketball court.
* **Indoor Games:** Facilities for table tennis (TT), carrom, chess, and shuttle badminton.
* **Gymnasium:** Fully equipped Fitness Center available for students with separate timings for boys and girls.

## 5. Canteen & Cafeteria
* **Keywords:** Canteen, Food, Lunch, Cafeteria, Snacks, Hygiene, Dining
* **Facility:** A large, hygienic on-campus canteen with modern seating and RO treated water.
* **Menu:** Offers affordable breakfast, lunch, snacks, and beverages with both South and North Indian options.

## 6. General Campus Amenities
* **Keywords:** Wi-Fi, Auditorium, Seminar Halls, Medical Facility, Health Center, Solar Power, Green Campus
* **Academic Amenities:** 24/7 Wi-Fi connectivity across academic blocks and labs, a large centralized auditorium, and multiple A/C Seminar Halls for events.
* **Health & Safety:** On-campus Health Center with first-aid facilities, a visiting doctor, and an emergency vehicle.
* **Green Initiatives:** Roof-top Solar Power Plant catering to the energy needs of the campus.
