## CSE — Academic Focus & Curriculum
* **Keywords:** CSE syllabus, CSE subjects, computer science curriculum, CSE core topics, BTech CSE subjects, software engineering, algorithms, data structures
* **Core Subjects:** Software Engineering, Algorithms & Data Structures, Object-Oriented Programming (Java, C++), Web Development (HTML, JS, React), Database Management Systems (SQL, NoSQL), Operating Systems, Computer Networks, and Cloud Computing.
* **Programs:** B.Tech (4 years) + M.Tech in CSE (2 years) focusing on Advanced Algorithms and Research Methodologies.

---

## ECE — Academic Focus & Curriculum
* **Keywords:** ECE syllabus, ECE subjects, electronics communication curriculum, ECE core topics, BTech ECE subjects, VLSI, embedded systems, signal processing
* **Core Subjects:** Analog Electronics, Digital Communications, Antenna Theory, IoT Integration, Signal Processing, Microprocessors & Microcontrollers (8086, ARM), and VLSI Design (FPGA, Chip Design).
* **Programs:** B.Tech (4 years) + M.Tech in VLSI Design (2 years) focusing on Chip Design, FPGA Implementation, and Low Power VLSI.

---

## EEE — Academic Focus & Curriculum
* **Keywords:** EEE syllabus, EEE subjects, electrical electronics curriculum, EEE core topics, BTech EEE subjects, power systems, control systems, electrical machines
* **Core Subjects:** Power Systems, Electrical Machines (DC/AC Motors, Transformers), Control Systems, Power Electronics (Drives, Converters), Renewable Energy (Solar, Wind), and Electric Vehicles (EV) Technology.
* **Programs:** B.Tech (4 years) + M.Tech in Power Electronics (2 years) focusing on Advanced Drives, Industrial Automation, and Renewable Energy Integration.

## Civil Engineering — Academic Focus & Curriculum
* **Keywords:** Civil syllabus, Civil subjects, civil engineering curriculum, Civil core topics, BTech Civil subjects, structural engineering, geotechnical, transportation
* **Core Subjects:** Structural Engineering (RCC Design, Steel Structures), Geotechnical Engineering (Soil Mechanics, Foundation Design), Transportation Engineering (Road Design, Bitumen Testing), Water Resources & Environmental Engineering (Water quality, Irrigation), Surveying (Total Station, GIS/AutoCAD), and Construction Management.

## Mechanical Engineering — Academic Focus & Curriculum
* **Keywords:** Mechanical syllabus, Mechanical subjects, mech curriculum, BTech Mechanical subjects, thermal engineering, robotics, CAD CAM, machine design
* **Core Subjects:** Thermal Engineering (IC Engine, Heat Transfer, Thermodynamics), Robotics & Automation (Humanoid Robotics, Industrial Automation), Manufacturing (Lathe, Milling, CNC), CAD/CAM (SolidWorks, ANSYS, CATIA, AutoCAD), Fluid Mechanics, and Material Science.
* **Programs:** B.Tech (4 years) + M.Tech in Machine Design (2 years) focusing on Advanced CAD/CAM, Vibration Analysis, and Material Science.

## AI&DS — Academic Focus & Curriculum
* **Keywords:** AI DS syllabus, AI DS subjects, artificial intelligence data science curriculum, AI DS core topics, BTech AI DS subjects, machine learning, deep learning, big data
* **Core Subjects:** Artificial Intelligence (Search Algorithms, Neural Networks, Fuzzy Logic), Data Science (Data Cleaning, Matplotlib/Seaborn, Statistical Analysis), Machine Learning (Supervised/Unsupervised, Regression, Clustering), Deep Learning (CNN, RNN, TensorFlow, Keras), and Big Data Analytics (Hadoop, Spark, NoSQL Databases).
* **Distinction from AI&ML:** AI&DS focuses on Data Analytics, Visualization, and Large-Scale Data Processing (Big Data), while AI&ML focuses on Algorithm Design, Robotics, and Deep Learning models.
* **Programs:** B.Tech (4 years) + M.Tech in AI&DS (2 years).

## AI&ML — Academic Focus & Curriculum
* **Keywords:** AI ML syllabus, AI ML subjects, artificial intelligence machine learning curriculum, AI ML core topics, BTech AI ML subjects, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, robotics
* **Core Subjects:** Machine Learning (Supervised/Unsupervised, Reinforcement Learning), Deep Learning (ANN, CNN, RNN, TensorFlow), Natural Language Processing (Text Mining, Speech Recognition, Chatbots), Computer Vision (Image Recognition, Object Detection), and Robotics (Hardware Integration).
* **Distinction from AI&DS:** AI&ML emphasizes Algorithm Design, Robotics, and Deep Learning Model Development, while AI&DS focuses on Data Analytics and Big Data pipelines.
* **Notable Projects:** Autonomous Vehicles, Disease Prediction Systems, Smart Surveillance.

## CSE – Cyber Security (CS) — Academic Focus & Curriculum
* **Keywords:** Cyber Security syllabus, CS subjects, cyber security curriculum, ethical hacking, cryptography, network security, cyber forensics, blockchain
* **Core Subjects:** Cryptography (AES/RSA Encryption, Digital Signatures), Network Security (Firewalls, VPNs, Intrusion Detection Systems), Ethical Hacking (Penetration Testing, Vulnerability Assessment), Cyber Forensics (Digital Evidence Recovery), and Blockchain Technology (Decentralized Secure Systems).
* **Unique Feature:** Virtual Cyber Security Lab — a sandboxed environment for hands-on Ethical Hacking training.

## FED (Freshman Engineering — I-Year) — Curriculum
* **Keywords:** FED syllabus, first year subjects, I-year curriculum, engineering physics, engineering chemistry, mathematics, CPDS, engineering graphics, communicative english
* **Core Subjects:** Mathematics (Linear Algebra/M1, Calculus/M2, Probability & Statistics), Engineering Physics (Applied Physics, Semiconductors), Engineering Chemistry, Communicative English & Soft Skills, C Programming & Data Structures (CPDS), and Engineering Graphics (Drawing & Visualization).
* **Labs:** English Communication Skills (ELCS) Lab, Engineering Physics Lab, Engineering Chemistry Lab, Engineering Workshop (Carpentry, Fitting, Tin Smithy), IT Workshop (PC Assembly, OS Installation, MS Office).
* **Note:** FED handles I-Year curriculum for ALL B.Tech branches. After I-Year, students proceed to their respective departments.
