# Fee Structure - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of Fee Structure
* **Keywords:** Fee Structure, RCEE Fees, College Fees, Tuition Cost, Payment Details, Financial Aid

## 1. Annual Tuition Fee
* **Keywords:** Annual Tuition Fee, BTech Fee, MTech Fee, MBA Fee, Convener Quota, Management Quota, APSCHE Tuition Fee
* **Category A (Convener Quota):** Fixed by the Government of Andhra Pradesh (APSCHE), the tuition fee is ₹60,200 per annum for B.Tech programs.
* **Category B (Management Quota):** Determined by the college management based on branch demand and student merit.

## 2. Hostel Fees (Boys & Girls)
* **Keywords:** Hostel Fees, Boys Hostel, Girls Hostel, Accommodation Cost, Mess Fee, Room Rent, Campus Facilities
* **Details:** Residential facilities are available with separate blocks for men and women, costing between ₹60,000 and ₹90,000 per year.
* **Inclusions:** The fee covers accommodation (AC or Non-AC options), mess (food), Wi-Fi, and general amenities, payable annually or semester-wise.

## 3. Transport Fees (Bus)
* **Keywords:** Transport Fees, Bus Fee, College Bus, Commute Charges, Transport Services, Route Charges
* **Details:** Optional transportation services cover Eluru, Vijayawada, and surrounding towns. Charges vary based on the specific boarding point distance and route, billed separately from tuition and hostel fees.

## 4. Caution Deposit
* **Keywords:** Caution Deposit, Refundable Deposit, Admission Deposit, Security Deposit, One Time Fee
* **Details:** A refundable caution deposit is collected once at the time of admission and is returned at the end of the course duration, subject to the clearance of dues or damages.

## 5. Fee Reimbursement (Government Schemes)
* **Keywords:** Fee Reimbursement, JVD, Jagananna Vidya Deevena, Vasathi Deevena, Tuition Waiver, Scholarship Eligibility, free seat BC caste, SC ST free admission, BC OBC fee exemption, government scholarship caste certificate, free tuition BC rank EAPCET
* **Schemes:** Eligible students can avail of the Jagananna Vidya Deevena (JVD) for full tuition fee reimbursement and Vasathi Deevena for hostel/food allowances.
* **Eligibility Criteria:** Requires a family annual income below ₹2.5 Lakhs, admission through the Category A (Convener) quota, maintenance of a minimum 75% attendance, and valid Income and Caste Certificates.

## 6. Ramachandra Merit Scholarship
* **Keywords:** Ramachandra Merit Scholarship, Merit Awards, Top Rankers, Fee Concession, Scholarship Pool, Laptops
* **Details:** The college offers a ₹50 Lakhs scholarship pool targeting top EAPCET/ICET rankers and academic toppers, providing incentives such as tuition or hostel fee concessions and potential laptop rewards.

## 7. Payment Schedule & Additional Charges
* **Keywords:** Payment Schedule, Exam Fees, Late Payment, Fines, Penalty, Due Dates
* **Details:** Tuition is payable annually at the commencement of the academic year, while exam fees are payable every semester prior to examinations. Late payments attract fines as per college regulations.
