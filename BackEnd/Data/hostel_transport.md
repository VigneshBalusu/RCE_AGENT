## 3. Hostel Facilities (Boys & Girls)
* **Keywords:** Hostel Facilities, Boys Hostel, Girls Hostel, Room Sharing, Amenities, Mess, Dining, Wi-Fi, RCEE Hostel
* **Room Configuration:** RCEE provides secure, separate hostels for boys and girls with 3-sharing or 4-sharing rooms, each equipped with a cot, cupboard, study table, and chair.
* **Amenities & Utilities:** Facilities include high-speed Wi-Fi, 24/7 RO mineral water, uninterrupted power backup, a common TV hall, indoor games, and gym access.
* **Mess & Dining:** The mess serves both vegetarian and non-vegetarian options, providing breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner.

## 4. Campus Safety & Mandatory Disclosures
* **Keywords:** Campus Safety, Anti-Ragging Policy, Mandatory Disclosures, AICTE Approved, IQAC, NIRF, RTI, NSS
* **Policies & Committees:** RCEE prioritizes student safety with a strict Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging policy actively monitored by a dedicated committee. The college is AICTE approved, participates in NIRF rankings, maintains academic standards via IQAC, complies with the RTI Act, and hosts an active NSS unit for student volunteering.
