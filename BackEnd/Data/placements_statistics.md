## 7. CSE Placements (Branch-Specific)
* **Keywords:** CSE Placements, CSE Highest Package, CSE Average Package, Top Recruiters CSE, Software Engineer Jobs, IT Placements CSE
* **Placement Statistics:** The CSE department maintains a robust placement record. Graduates secure average packages ranging from 3.5 LPA to 6.5 LPA. The highest packages for top performers typically range between 6 LPA and above.
* **Top Recruiters & Roles:** Major recruiters include TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Cognizant. Students are placed in roles like Software Developer, Systems Engineer, Data Analyst, and IT Consultant.

## 8. ECE Placements (Branch-Specific)
* **Keywords:** ECE Placements, ECE Jobs, VLSI Jobs, Embedded Jobs, ECE Salary, Core Electronics, Dual Advantage ECE
* **Placement Statistics:** ECE students possess a "Dual Advantage," eligible for both core electronics and software/IT roles. Recent numbers: 116 placements (2021–22), 110 (2022–23), 117 (2023–24) — steady 70%+ placement rate. Average package ₹3.5–₹4.5 LPA, highest up to ₹6 LPA at Accenture and TCS Digital.
* **Top Recruiters:** TCS (Digital & NINJA), Accenture, Infosys, Wipro, Capgemini, DXC Technologies, HCL, Sonata Software, AiFA Labs, Savantis Solutions.

## 9. EEE Placements (Branch-Specific)
* **Keywords:** EEE Placements, EEE Jobs, Core Companies EEE, EEE Salary, Top Recruiters EEE, Voltech, Efftronics
* **Placement Statistics:** EEE students secure roles in both core electrical firms and IT companies. Placement rate ~75–80%. Median salary ₹3.5–₹4 LPA, highest packages up to ₹5 LPA.
* **Top Recruiters:** Core: Voltech, Petrofac, Efftronics, Amar Raja Batteries. IT: TCS, Capgemini, Tech Mahindra, Wipro.
