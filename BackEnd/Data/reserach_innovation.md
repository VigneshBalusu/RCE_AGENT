# Research, Innovation & Startups - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of Research & Innovation
* **Keywords:** Research, Innovation, Startups, R&D, Tech Development, RCEE Research

## 1. Research & Development (R&D) Cell
* **Keywords:** R&D Cell, Research and Development, Dr. Jarabala Ranga, Patents, Publications, AI, EV, IoT, Smart Grids, Blockchain
* **Leadership & Focus:** Headed by Dr. Jarabala Ranga, the R&D Cell focuses on Artificial Intelligence (AI), Electric Vehicles (EV), Smart Grids, Internet of Things (IoT), and Blockchain Technology.
* **Key Achievements:** Faculty and students have successfully filed 20+ Indian Patents and secured 5+ UK Grants, with active research frequently published in prestigious IEEE Xplore and SCI journals.

## 2. Innovation Centers & Labs
* **Keywords:** Innovation Centers, AICTE IDEA Lab, Prototyping, NewGen IEDC, IIC, Institution Innovation Council, Student Entrepreneurs
* **AICTE IDEA Lab:** Funded by a substantial AICTE grant of approximately ₹1.10 Crore, this dedicated manufacturing and prototyping facility allows students to convert theoretical ideas into physical products.
* **NewGen IEDC & IIC:** The Institution’s Innovation Council (IIC) provides funding support, mentorship, and a platform for budding student entrepreneurs.

## 3. Funded Projects & Grants
* **Keywords:** Funded Projects, Grants, DST, AICTE Schemes, MODROBS, FDP, Rural Sustainable Development
* **DST Collaborations:** Ongoing Department of Science & Technology (DST) projects focus on Rural Sustainable Development and Blockchain implementation.
* **AICTE Schemes:** The college actively secures funding through MODROBS (Modernization and Removal of Obsolescence) and FDP (Faculty Development Programs) to upskill staff and upgrade technical facilities.

## 4. Student Opportunities
* **Keywords:** Student Opportunities, Startup Incubation, Hackathons, Smart India Hackathon, SIH, Entrepreneurship
* **Startup Incubation:** Students can register their startups through the college's Incubation Center to receive dedicated logistical and technical support.
* **Hackathons:** Students regularly and successfully participate in national-level competitive events like the Smart India Hackathon (SIH).
