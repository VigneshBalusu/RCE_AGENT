# Student Life, Clubs & Cultural Events - Ramachandra College of Engineering (RCEE)

## Overview of Student Life
* **Keywords:** Student Life, Campus Events, Cultural Activities, Extracurriculars, RCEE Fests, Student Organizations

## 1. Annual Festivals (Fests)
* **Keywords:** Fests, Cultural Activities, KREYA, Parichay, Freshers Day, Sankranthi Sambaralu, Tech Fests, Campus Events, College Fest
* **KREYA:** The flagship National Level Cultural & Technical Fest, typically held in March or April, featuring dance, music, tech expos, and fashion shows.
* **Campus Celebrations:** Other major events include Parichay (the official Freshers' Day to welcome new students), Sankranthi Sambaralu (a grand traditional celebration featuring Rangoli competitions and ethnic wear), and department-specific technical symposia like "Talent Explore" by CSE.

## 2. Student Clubs
* **Keywords:** Clubs, Student Clubs, Robotics Club, Coding Club, Cultural Clubs, Women Empowerment, IETE, FOSS
* **Technical Clubs:** Active organizations include the Robotics Club (conducting hands-on workshops on Arduino and IoT), the Coding Club / FOSS (focusing on Open Source Software contributions and Hackathon preparation), and the IETE Student Forum (specialized workshops for Electronics and Communication).
* **Cultural & Social Clubs:** Includes the Rani Rudrama Devi Club (dedicated to promoting culture, arts, and traditions), the Mother Theresa Social Club (focusing on charity, empathy, and social service initiatives), and the Kalpana Chawla Women Empowerment Cell (organizing events and workshops specifically for female students and staff).

## 3. Social Service (NSS)
* **Keywords:** NSS, Social Service, Volunteering, Blood Donation, Swachh Bharat, Medical Camps, Community Service, National Service Scheme
* **Activities & Benefits:** The active National Service Scheme (NSS) unit organizes regular Blood Donation Camps, Village Adoption programs, Swachh Bharat drives, and Medical Camps. Participants earn social credits and certificates, adding value to their overall profile.

## 4. Professional Chapters
* **Keywords:** IEEE, CSI, Professional Networks, Global Chapters, Computer Society, Engineering Networks
* **Global & National Networks:** Students have access to global professional networks through the IEEE Student Branch, which connects them with the engineering community for networking and standards, and the CSI (Computer Society of India), which focuses on professional development for CSE and IT students.

---

## 5. Department-Specific Activities & Competitions
* **Keywords:** department activities, branch events, hackathon, competition, department club, technical association, HUNT ECE, VID YOUTH EEE, RACE Civil, AI hackathon, CSE events, Mech events, SIH, Smart India Hackathon, MBA events

* **CSE:** FOSS (Free and Open Source Software) club focusing on open-source contributions and hackathon preparation; department symposia like "Talent Explore".

* **AI&DS:** Active participation in **Smart India Hackathon (SIH)** and **Kaggle** competitions; hands-on workshops on Generative AI, Data Visualization, AWS Machine Learning Specialty, and NPTEL certifications.

* **AI&ML:** Workshops on deploying ML models on Cloud and Generative AI (ChatGPT/Gemini) architecture; student projects including Autonomous Vehicles, Disease Prediction Systems, and Smart Surveillance.

* **ECE:** **HUNT Association** (Home of Unlimited Novel Technologies) — founded 2010; organizes technical quizzes, circuit idea expos, and poster presentations. Active IETE and ISTE student chapters.

* **EEE:** **VID-YOUTH** (formerly E-STAR) technical association — organizes PCB Design workshops, "IoT Driven Solutions" and "AI in Power Sector" seminars, and industrial visits (e.g., Satish Dhawan Space Centre, SHAR).

* **Civil:** **RACE** (Royal Association of Civil Engineering) — organizes technical fests, Model Expos, and Engineer's Day. Publishes the "ABHIGYAN" student newsletter. Workshops on Total Station and Revit Architecture.

* **Mechanical:** ISTE, IAENG, and SDIWC active chapters; annual **Project Expos** featuring student-built Go-Karts and Robots; Design Thinking workshops.

* **Cyber Security:** Virtual Lab workshops on Ethical Hacking, live Cyber Attack simulations, and Capture-the-Flag (CTF) events.

* **IoT:** **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Hardware Edition** participation; Smart Agriculture and Industrial Automation prototype showcases at annual Project Expos; Robotics Integration workshops.

* **MBA:** Management Meets featuring B-Quiz, Young Manager contests, and **MarketKshetra** marketing expo. Industrial visits to Coca-Cola, Sangam Dairy, and Vizag Steel Plant. Soft Skills and Aptitude training from Semester 1.
